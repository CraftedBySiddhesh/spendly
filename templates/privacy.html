Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic log data such as pages visited and timestamps, used to maintain and improve the Service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to operate and improve Spendly:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.
  • To communicate service-related updates (no marketing emails without your consent).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for any commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon a valid deletion request, your data will be removed within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use trusted third-party providers for hosting and infrastructure. These providers process your data only on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

We do not integrate third-party advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics services that share data outside of our infrastructure.

External links within the app (if any) are governed by the privacy policies of their respective owners. We are not responsible for their practices.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses session cookies to keep you logged in while you use the app. These cookies are strictly necessary for the Service to function and are not used for tracking or advertising purposes. They expire when you log out or close your browser session.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided within the app. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly.
